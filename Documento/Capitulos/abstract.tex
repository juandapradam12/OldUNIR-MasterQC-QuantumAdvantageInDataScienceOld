\chapter{Abstract}

Quantum computing represents an emerging paradigm with transformative potential for data science. However, a significant gap persists between the theoretical promises of quantum algorithms and their practical applicability in real business environments. This work proposes a rigorous comparative study between quantum algorithms —specifically quantum kernel methods and the QAOA— and their classical counterparts —SVM, Random Forests and optimization heuristics— applied to real-world supervised classification and combinatorial optimization problems, using data from a private company. Performance is evaluated through accuracy, computational efficiency and scalability metrics. Additionally, a conceptual hybrid quantum-classical workflow prototype is designed, oriented towards practical integration into production pipelines. The results aim to provide empirical evidence on the real conditions of quantum advantage in applied data science.

\textbf{Keywords:} quantum computing, data science, quantum advantage, quantum machine learning, QAOA
